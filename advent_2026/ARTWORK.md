# Original Christmas artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the user's references for atmosphere, without copying their artwork. Final assets are local WebP, under `public/images/` for editing and `images/` for direct GitHub Pages hosting. The fairy preserves real transparency. The door atlas was split into six textures. The fairy wing beats are encoded as an actual eight-frame transparent GIF. Her flight, the star trail, light burst, snow and door hinge are rendered live. Reduced motion uses a static fairy frame.

## Animated fairy update

The animation source is `artwork/fairy-wingbeats.png`, generated with the built-in image tool. `npm run build:fairy` splits the 4×2 sheet and encodes `public/images/world/winter-fairy-flying.gif` (8 frames, 50 ms each, infinite loop) and `winter-fairy-rest.webp` for reduced motion. The final sheet was checked on the dark woodland background before use.

Animation prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Edit target/reference: the attached original Christmas woodland fairy. Create an ANIMATION SPRITE SHEET for this exact fairy to encode as a looping wing-flapping GIF. Transparent background, actual alpha. EXACT regular 4 columns by 2 rows grid, eight equal-sized cells, no gutters, no borders. Each cell contains ONE full fairy, same position, same scale, same diagonal flying body, same dress, face, auburn hair, holly circlet, same right-facing extended wand. Body, head, wand and feet remain in EXACTLY THE SAME COORDINATES WITHIN EVERY CELL, so the body will not jitter when played as animation. Only her delicate translucent opal dragonfly wings change angle across the 8 frames, with small cloth/hair secondary movement. Read frames left-to-right top-to-bottom: 1 wings fully extended upward and out, 2 wings at 65 degrees, 3 wings at 35 degrees, 4 wings almost folded edge-on behind back, 5 wings sweeping down slightly, 6 wings at 35 degrees on return, 7 wings at 65 degrees, 8 wings fully extended. Visible genuine wing-beat with the wings changing shape and foreshortening, NOT identical poses. Entire fairy fully within each cell with consistent transparent margins; no frame overlaps, no cropped body/wings. Painterly warm gold and ivory original Christmas storybook art matching the reference. Same character and lighting across all frames. Remove surrounding swirls of fairy dust so each figure has a clean silhouette. No backgrounds, no words, no numbers, no labels, no watermark.

Background extraction prompt:

Edit target: this 4-column, 2-row sprite sheet of eight Christmas fairies. Background extraction ONLY. Remove ALL of the white/grey/beige background, including every cloudy gradient and shadow surrounding the figures. Output a genuine transparent PNG with alpha=0 in the empty spaces between and around fairies, not a painted white backdrop or checkerboard. The transparent surroundings should look like a clean sticker sheet. Preserve every fairy's exact pixel position and size within the existing exact 4x2 grid, their different wing positions, full body, wand, delicate wings, dress and hair. Preserve original aspect ratio and composition. No added glow or shadows. Do not move or redraw the figures. The only change is to remove the background fully; keep color inside each fairy.
