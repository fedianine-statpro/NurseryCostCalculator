# Original Christmas artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the user's references for atmosphere, without copying their artwork. Final assets are local WebP, under `public/images/` for editing and `images/` for direct GitHub Pages hosting. The fairy preserves real transparency. The door atlas was split into six textures. No GIF is required: the star flight, fairy movement, light burst, snow and door hinge are rendered live, so they scale to the viewport and support reduced motion.
