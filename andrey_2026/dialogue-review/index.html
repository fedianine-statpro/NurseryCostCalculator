Birthday quest: dialogue review

Approved review, implemented on 16 October 2026. Before columns preserve the original dialogue. 38 main-script entries and 39 additional review entries, with the current and proposed versions side by side.

The revision gives characters different concerns, lets actions deliver their own surprises, and removes explanations after punchlines. Some scenery and personal memories should simply be clear, quiet, or warm. Square brackets are staging notes, not recorded dialogue.

See the full editorial notes and reference research for the approach and scope.
