# Audio sources and licences

All 29 new assets are local files. The nine compositions and the Irina resolution are original offline arrangements of the shared D-major motif, rendered using recorded acoustic instrument samples. No existing song, streaming service, narration or runtime synthesizer is used. Family recordings retain their separate private family provenance and are unchanged.

## Instrument recordings (CC0-1.0)

- [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE): Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. Quiet upright piano, harp, cello pizzicato, violin, claves and conga. See the repository LICENSE. Downloads are pinned to the Git tree recorded in the production source cache.
- [FreePats Spanish classical guitar](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html): Roberto, version 20190618; real nylon guitar recorded in 2008. CC0, as stated in its readme and cc0.txt. Used throughout the music and the warm resolution.

These are adapted physical recordings: trimmed, filtered, gently levelled, sometimes slowed or layered. The tape measure uses a cloth/belt handling source; stamp sounds use softened physical impacts. Lakeside ambience uses water only, without added bird calls. These environmental recordings supply atmosphere, not biographical location evidence. No continuous artificial crackle is added.

CC0 permits redistribution and adaptation without attribution requirements; creators and primary sources are retained here voluntarily. [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode). Source notices and lossless masters are archived outside the published site. Exact edits, levels, file hashes and durations are in production-report.json; individual source edits and the reproducible score renderer are in tools/produce-audio.cjs.
