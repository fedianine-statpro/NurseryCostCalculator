Our condition guide

Pre-loved means pre-loved. Some signs of school life are normal, so we spell them out before you buy.

Good

Good

Clearly pre-loved and fully wearable. May have small pen or marker marks, light staining, fading or bobbling, plus a previous name inside.

Spare

Spare

Heavily used but wearable. May have obvious marks, fading, repairs or significant bobbling. Prices can be as low as £1.

What about nearly-new items? We do not advertise a separate “Excellent” grade because those pieces are too rare to promise. If you receive an unusually pristine item, enjoy the upgrade at the normal Good price.

Before you order

Good to know

Will I receive the item shown in the picture?

No. Product illustrations and condition examples are representative only. You will receive the same garment type, size and condition grade you selected, but not the exact item pictured. Occasionally a Good item may be in better-than-expected condition.

Can pre-loved items have stains or writing?

Yes. Good and Spare items may have permanent-marker or pen marks, fading, staining, bobbling or a previous child’s name inside. The condition guide explains what is acceptable in each condition.

What do you reject?

We do not sell items that are unhygienic, have major holes, unusable fastenings or damage that makes them unsuitable for normal wear.

What if I’m unhappy with my item?

Bring it back to the main office and, where availability allows, the PTA can swap it for another item of the same type, size and condition.

When can I collect?

Collections are normally from the main school office on Fridays during term time. Please allow at least 3 days for PTA volunteers to prepare your order. Collection arrangements may vary around school holidays or special events.

Why buy second-hand?

It keeps usable uniform in circulation, reduces costs for families and raises money for Friends of Poplar Primary PTA.
